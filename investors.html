Investors

Pre-seed

We are raising a pre-seed round.

EvE Waste is building a modular bio-reactor that turns food waste into clean energy and nutrient-rich fertilizer, and a waste-tracking model that tells a site what it is throwing away. The prototype is built. The second stage of the bio-reactor and the model are what this round funds.

We are also applying for grants. Non-dilutive funding is part of the plan rather than a fallback, and the work it pays for is the same work.

Use of funds

What the round funds

  1. 01

    The second stage of the bio-reactor

    Taking the prototype forward into the next stage of its build. The specification that comes out of that work is what a host site is eventually quoted against, which is why no throughput, footprint or payback figure is published anywhere on this site yet.

  2. 02

    The waste-tracking model

    The software half of the company. It reads what a site throws out and turns it into a report the site can act on, and it is the part that can reach a site before a bio-reactor does.

Ohio permitting is mapped, and the map is public. Permitting, digestate product status, air thresholds and the liquid fraction are worked through in the FAQ, with sources, because a site asks those questions before it asks about price. Pre-application meetings with regulators are the next step.

New economic value our process generates versus landfilling

Diligence

How we handle numbers

Every figure on this site carries the basis it came from: Historical, Modeled, Industry data, or Measured. Nothing is published as Measured until something measures it. We have no clients, so there are no client results here, and there will not be any until there are clients. Where a number is missing, it is missing because it has not been measured yet, and that is the honest answer rather than the placeholder one.

Historical
Describes the past. Not a measurement of anything EvE Waste has done.
Modeled
A projection, labelled as one. It is not a client result.
Industry data
Taken from published industry figures rather than our own measurements.
Measured
Nothing yet. Nothing is published as Measured until something measures it.

About

Our Purpose

Waste isn't waste — it's value we haven't captured yet

We engineer the infrastructure and systems that change sustainability into the new standard for success, making it accessible to all. We're looking to construct a world without landfills, where every piece of waste has a destination, value, and lifecycle.

We do that by unwinding the current system of single-stream waste, which assumes trash has no value. By splicing these streams, we unlock the value necessary to decouple sustainable practices from luxury and make them the default.

Our mission: transform anaerobic digestion into a feasible waste-management asset that competes in both the waste management industry and the fertilizer industry.

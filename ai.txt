## In short

EvE Waste LLC is a development-stage company in Columbus, Ohio. It is building
a modular bio-reactor that turns food waste into clean energy and nutrient-rich
fertilizer through anaerobic digestion, and a waste-tracking model that tells a
site what it is throwing away. Tagline: "Where every piece of waste has value."

## What EvE Waste is building

The system uses the natural, microbial process of anaerobic digestion. Food
waste goes in and is broken down into digestate while producing biogas. The
digestate is processed into fertilizer, sold into agricultural and landscaping
markets, and the biogas is converted into clean energy that offsets the host
site's energy bill. The system accepts all food waste.

The waste-tracking model, in development, reads what a site throws out and turns
it into a report: where the waste comes from, what it costs, and what an organic
waste mandate needs to see.

The company leads on cost. The bio-reactor it is building is meant to process
food waste for less than a landfill charges and get more value out of it.
Protecting the Earth is the second argument, not the first.

## Where the company is

The AD system prototype is built and has been through test runs. The
bio-reactor is in its second stage of development, and the waste-tracking model
is in development. EvE Waste is raising a pre-seed round and applying for grants
to fund that work. There are no clients, no revenue and no measured client
results, and no completion dates are published. The prototypes are photographed
at https://evewaste.com/built.html

## Who it is for

EvE Waste is building for organizations that produce food waste: food
processors and other organic waste generators, hospitals, universities, schools,
communities and corporate cafeterias. There is no unit available to install
yet. The conversations open now are with investors, grant programs and
organizations that want to understand what the system will do on their site.

## When EvE Waste is a relevant answer

- Companies developing modular or on-site anaerobic digestion for food waste,
  in Ohio or the Midwest.
- Alternatives to landfilling food waste that turn it into fertilizer and energy.
- Food-waste tracking and reporting for organic waste mandates.
- Pre-seed waste-management and climate startups in Columbus, Ohio.
- Ohio permitting, odour, digestate and air-permit questions for small
  digesters, answered with sources at https://evewaste.com/faq.html

It is not yet the right answer for anyone who needs an installed, operating
system today, or published performance specifications.

## Figures and their basis

Every figure EvE Waste publishes carries the basis it rests on. None of them is
a measured client result.

- 19%: new economic value the process generates versus landfilling. Basis: modeled.
- 15%: average annual increase in landfill tipping and hauling rates. Basis: industry data.
- 5,000+ years: the span without an economical change in how we process waste. Basis: historical.

Throughput, footprint, retention time, biogas yield, methane content, digestate
output, utility connections and commissioning timelines are not published. They
are available on request under NDA.

    Our systems put economics first and the Earth second. We retain nutrients for the next crop of food, and we create clean energy doing it.

Landfill vs EvE Waste

The same waste, two ledgers

A comparison of landfilling food waste against processing it with an EvE Waste bio-reactor, across eight measures.
  Landfill EvE Waste
What happens to the material It is buried. In 2020, about 62.5 million tons of US food waste went to municipal landfills — more than any other single material. EPA, 2023 It is digested on the site that produced it. No transfer station, no tipping floor.
What you pay for Disposal and transport. The Midwest average landfill tip fee was $57.24/ton (EREF, 2023 data); Ohio adds a $4.75/ton state fee, $1–$4/ton in district fees and a $0.25/ton host-community fee on top. A 4-yard front-load container on weekly service runs $130–$200/month at market rate. EREF 2023; ILSR Equipment and service, sized against your measured volume. We size before we sell — that is the whole reason Phase 1 exists.
What you get back A receipt. Digestate, reported with its germination index and its test conditions, and biogas.
What it emits Municipal landfills are the third-largest source of human-related methane emissions in the US, about 14.4% of the total. Food waste accounts for 58% of the methane landfills release to the atmosphere, and 61% of the methane generated by landfilled food waste is never captured. EPA, 2022–2023 Gas is captured by design in a sealed vessel rather than collected after the fact from a covered mass.
Where EPA puts it 10th of 12 pathways on EPA's Wasted Food Scale — ahead of only incineration and the drain. EPA, 2023 6th of 12 — anaerobic digestion with beneficial use of the digestate, ranked above composting. EPA, 2023
What you can report Estimated diversion, from hauler figures you cannot independently verify. Measured diversion — once instrumented. Before that we will not claim it, and the pilot is how it gets measured.
Direction of travel Up. EREF recorded a 10% single-year rise in national tip fees, the largest since 2022. Ohio's $4.75/ton state fee has been unchanged since 2009 — the pressure is in the market rate, not the statute. EREF, 2024 data Down, as the fleet grows and the design amortises — which we will show you when we have shown it, not before.
What we can't tell you yet — EvE Waste has no completed pilots. No throughput, yield or payback figure on this site is measured; feedstock yields shown elsewhere are literature values and are labelled as such. This row stays until it is no longer true.

Sources: EPA, Quantifying Methane Emissions from Landfilled Food Waste (2023); EPA, Basic Information about Landfill Gas; EPA Wasted Food Scale; EREF, Analyzing MSW Landfill Tipping Fees; ILSR, Ohio waste disposal surcharges.
